## Recognition

- Contributions are listed after they have been reviewed and merged.
- The entry describes the work that was merged, not a general endorsement or employment relationship.
- Contributors can request a preferred public attribution or correction by opening an Issue.
- We may highlight merged contributions in project updates. We will not imply that a contributor endorses Motech Audit.

Thank you for helping make smart-contract security education more practical and reproducible.
